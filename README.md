# Pakeezah Atelier — A way of living

A complete static website. Open dist/index.html or serve/deploy dist. vercel.json selects the correct static output. No dependencies, install or build step are required.

## Visual direction
Deep burgundy, dusty rose, warm paper and charcoal. Oversized Manrope display type pairs with Bodoni Moda italics. The opening combines a geometric-floor entrance image and layered typography, with an early mobile brief action. Only the hero and selected-work heading retain the largest display scale. The portfolio is an asymmetric editorial spread with large image numerals. Original films are presented in a dark documentary section near the top. Material images have an optional pointer magnifier and open in a full-image dialog on tap, click or keyboard activation.

## Architectural opening
A short, original SVG room assembly: the plan draws, geometric stone fills in, timber and wall planes rise, then sunlight crosses the floor. The assembly resolves into the actual entrance photograph, which moves into the hero’s measured position as the burgundy panels part. The opening is deliberately paced: drawing, wall assembly and a light sweep are followed by a short hold of the finished room, the photographic dissolve and the doorway reveal. Normal entry takes about 5.55 seconds; a slow or failed image has a bounded exit at approximately 5.75 seconds. Skip and Escape close immediately, including during departure. The headline animation resumes during the reveal. It runs once per browser session. Deep links, back navigation and reduced-motion visits bypass it. Enter the atelier and Escape skip immediately; Replay opening is in the footer. Image preparation has a bounded wait and an independent fail-open safeguard. No simulated percentage or video preload is used.

## Functionality
All five study collections and all six films open in a native dialog with keyboard focus restoration. Each study contains specific visual observations and descriptive captions. The entrance has three keyboard-accessible, synchronised detail controls; mobile image markers scroll to the selected explanation. Film titles, context, silent-film labels and measured runtimes appear in the page and viewer. The full-screen navigation supports focus containment and Escape. The material selectors work without hover. Tap or click the selected image to open its complete view; Enter and Space do the same from keyboard focus. The magnifier is an additional desktop interaction. Reduced motion disables entrance animation, parallax and the lens. No content depends on pinned scrolling; the page is naturally scrollable. Mobile compositions have separate image sizing and placement. The mobile hero uses natural document flow, shows the brief action before the photograph and retains a header contact link. The hero’s circular study link sits outside the image mask. Navigation follows the five page chapters consistently.
